# Sunrise Point · 2023 vision demo

This folder holds a website concept for The Inn at Sunrise Point (the "2023
vision", modeled on the Gregøri Webflow hotel template) and the brief for
turning it into a demo video. The full brief is in PROMPT.md: read it before
doing anything.

- `site/` is the finished concept. Treat its copy, prices, placeholders and
  photos as final; fix only rendering bugs, and say what you changed.
- Write all generated files to `out/`. Keep scratch work in `out/tmp/` and
  delete it once the videos are verified.
- Brand: sandy gold #D4AF37, white #FFFFFF, black #111111. Fonts: Georgia
  (headlines, system font) and Open Sans (text, from Google Fonts).
