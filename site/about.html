About us

Our story

Hosted by Matt & MJ Ruffing

It was love at first sight, in the middle of winter

Matt and MJ first drove down the lane when the inn was closed for the season. They took over in the spring of 2021 and have hosted every season since, at breakfast, in the gardens and out on the lawn at dawn.

They've kept what guests have loved for three decades and added their own touches: seasonal tulip plantings, herbs and edible flowers for breakfast, and a warm welcome at the door.
