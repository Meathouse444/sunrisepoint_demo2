Our philosophy

A quiet promise: nothing to do but watch the sun come up.

The Inn at Sunrise Point sits on five acres of private shoreline overlooking Penobscot Bay. We keep it small on purpose: twelve rooms and cottages, each with a fireplace, a private deck and a view worth waking up for.

Our promise is simple. Sophisticated comfort without fuss, a gourmet breakfast to start the day, and hosts who know the coast and will help you plan as much, or as little, as you like.

About the inn

Unwind in style on the Maine coast

Breakfast in the glass conservatory. An afternoon in the gardens, where tulips give way to peonies and dahlias. Evenings by your own fire, with the bay turning gold outside.

Hosted by Matt and MJ Ruffing since 2021, the inn is ten minutes north of Camden's harbor, close enough for dinner in town and far enough for real quiet.
