Amenities

Simple. Perfect. Luxury.

Every morning

Gourmet breakfast in the conservatory

A full breakfast is included with every stay, served in the glass conservatory or brought to your room. Herbs and edible flowers come from our own gardens, and our chef will happily cook around any dietary needs.

Fresh-baked cookies, fruit and chocolates are out all afternoon.

Five acres

Gardens from the tulips to the tideline

Spring opens with tulips, summer fills every bed with peonies, foxgloves and dahlias, and a path leads through it all to the water. Cut flowers from the garden find their way into every room.

Garden Spa

Massage in the garden, or by your own fire

Professional therapists, in our Garden Spa or in your room. Schedules fill quickly in summer, so reserve when you book.

The shoreline

A private stretch of Penobscot Bay

Walk down to the rocky shore at dawn, stack a cairn, or settle into an Adirondack chair with a glass of wine and watch the lobster boats come home.

Evenings

Books, art and a well-stocked bar

Our library holds books, games and local art, with a curated selection of wine and spirits. Our concierge knows the coast well and can book sails, dinners and spa time for you.
